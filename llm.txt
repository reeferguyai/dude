# ReeferDude.com & PotGrowHub.store

> Zero-gravity WebXR spatial commerce universe, voice-controlled AI virtual budtender (GanjaGuru / GanjaGoddessAI), and zero-inventory Print-on-Demand (POD) & 3D Print-on-Demand (3DPoD) ecosystem powered by the Gravity-Gone physics engine.

ReeferDude (`https://reeferdude.com`) and PotGrowHub (`https://potgrowhub.store`) form an interconnected spatial web platform built on a zero-inventory "Socket" architecture. Users navigate a weightless cosmos centered around **GanjaGroot** (the living botanical hub) by grabbing, dragging, or flicking luminous smoke portals across 2D physics, 3D WebGL, and WebXR AR/VR modes.

## Core Ecosystem Entities

- **ReeferDude (`https://reeferdude.com`)**: Primary spatial web application, interactive cosmos, Dream-It Studio, Knowledge Grove, and gamified SmokeSeeds rewards hub.
- **PotGrowHub (`https://potgrowhub.store`)**: Sustainable e-commerce storefront specializing in zero-inventory hemp apparel, biodegradable 3DPoD accessories, and verified eco-friendly lifestyle gear.
- **GanjaGuru / GanjaGoddessAI (`https://ganjagoddessai.com`)**: Voice-and-text conversational AI assistant supporting Web Speech API, offline IndexedDB RAG, and local GGUF/Ollama models.
- **Gravity-Gone Engine**: Modular pointer-gesture, momentum/flick physics, and adaptive rendering engine scaling from mobile PWAs to desktop WebGL and WebXR headsets.
